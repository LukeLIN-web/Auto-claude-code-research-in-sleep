# Revised PDF: what the paper may change

Read before any edit to the paper for a revised PDF (venues that allow one) and before pruning a rebuttal appendix. Every paper edit is a checklist item of `REVISION_PLAN.md` (`SKILL.md` Phase 4); an error no reviewer raised goes to its Unraised errors section.

- **A revised PDF edits only what a reviewer raised** (user 2026-11-07: 「论文不要乱改, 只改 reviewer 提出的. 没提的错误就写到内部文档」). Every change between the submitted version and the revised PDF traces to a checklist item, and through it to an `issue_id`. An error no reviewer raised goes to the Unraised errors section, not into the paper, even when the fix is one word and sits next to an edit being made. Before the revised PDF goes to the user, diff it against the submitted version and match every hunk to an item.
- **Keep the revision small; every new appendix is a cost** (user 2026-11-13: 「附录不能加太多, 加太多了 reviewer会认为改动太大, 拒绝叫你投别的会议. 能不改就不改.」). A revision that reads like a different paper invites "too many changes, resubmit elsewhere". Answer in the reply text by default. A new or longer appendix needs a reviewer who asked for that content and a reply that cannot carry it. Never add one to pre-empt a question. Inside a rebuttal appendix keep only what some reply cites (its numbers, tables, design facts) plus what defines a measure those parts use. Design-summary tables, threats-to-validity lists, allocation figures, demographic-balance checks, literature justifications, duplicate counts and extra per-pair tables go. When pruning, grep every reply for "Appendix <letter>" and for each table/figure label, then comment out (`% `) rather than delete, so co-author text stays recoverable (user 2026-11-13「你把合作者的不必要的注释一些」). Rebuild and confirm no undefined references.
- **Paper and replies do not repeat each other** (user 2026-11-13: 「论文内容不要和 comment 重复，讲了，论文就不用加」). What a reply already states (design steps, sample sizes, numbers, findings, the IRB sentence) is not also written into the revised PDF; the rebuttal appendix keeps only what the replies point to and do not carry themselves, plus what a kept table needs to be read (its caption terms, the scoring rule). Citations that support only rebuttal-added text go in the reply as author-year ("(<Author> & <Author>, <year>; <Author> et al., <year>)", 「这些参考文献也隐藏了。加到 response 里去」), not into the paper's bibliography, so the revised reference list matches the submission's; hide the `\citep` with a `% ` note that keeps it. After pruning: (1) grep every reply for sentences that say what the revised manuscript contains ("We have added …", "Appendix N shows …", "two of the nine baselines in Appendix N") and confirm each still holds in the pruned PDF, or ask the user whether to restore the sentence in the paper or drop the claim from the reply; (2) a hidden clause must not leave a dangling structure ("three steps. First, …" with no Second/Third) or a `\Cref` to a hidden label; (3) every revision mark uses the one revision colour (`\rev{}`), never a co-author's leftover `\textcolor{blue}`.
- **Add a requested baseline to a submitted table only if it causes no problem** (user 2026-11-13, on a reviewer asking for one more baseline next to the two in a results table: 「rebuttal 都别改, 不要改 table ⟨n⟩, 她要的东西就在 comment 里面说」, then 「也不是不加, 主要是加了会出问题就别加. 没问题也可以加.」). The reply always carries the numbers. Before also adding rows or columns to a table of the submitted paper, check what the addition touches: sentences elsewhere that count or describe that table ("all nine baselines of Table N"), other tables that list the same methods and would now look incomplete ("why is it not in Table M too?"), and whether filling those in would print a metric we lose on. If any of these breaks or invites a weakness, keep the table as submitted and answer in the comment only, and the reply does not say "we have added … to Table N". If nothing breaks, adding it is fine. The decision goes to the user with what was checked.
- **Internal evidence that undercuts a paper sentence waits for camera-ready** (same ruling). When a new experiment shows a printed sentence is overstated or inaccurate and no reviewer raised it (e.g. a "bounded by label noise" claim that a new baseline exceeds), leave the sentence in the revised PDF, keep it out of every reply, and list the exact fix under a camera-ready item in `REVISION_PLAN.md`. A red \\rev{} edit there would point reviewers at it. Once every such conflict is ruled on, delete the conflict section from the status board (settled items leave the board).
