# Phase 5 lints 8–26

Run at Phase 5 together with lints 1–7 of `SKILL.md`, and again where a later step names a lint by number (Phase 6.5, Phase 8).

8. **Adversarial design-choice scan** — for each experimental claim, ask: "Could a hostile reviewer find a non-obvious design choice (compute-match, frozen subset, sampling protocol) that I haven't disclosed?" If yes and the choice is a neutral setup fact, add one line to the Setup paragraph; if stating it would expose a weakness, leave it out (`SKILL.md` Phase 4 "Never expose a weakness"). Narrower than provenance; focused on *design choices* not factual sources.
9. **Cross-thread duplication** (`per_reviewer_thread` mode only) — compare reviewer files pairwise: any sentence of ~15+ words appearing near-verbatim in more than one file, outside the `SETUP_METRICS_BLOCK.md` inclusion, fails. Fix by keeping the full treatment in the primary thread and recomposing the other occurrences per the shared-evidence recipe (`SKILL.md` Phase 4).
10. **Weakness and concession scan** — flag every sentence or table, in numbers or in words, that breaks `SKILL.md` Phase 4 "Never expose a weakness" or "Never concede" (a change the reviewer explicitly requested, reported in the reviewer's own verb, is that rule's exception and passes), and every timing excuse, lineage word, run date or unearned "slower" / "faster" ([rhetoric.md](rhetoric.md) § 4, C27–C29). Fix per those rules.
11. **Thanks-opener scan** — every `A#` opens with at most two short sentences of thanks (warm tone and an exclamation mark are fine), and the very next sentence is the answer. Flag: answers with no thanks, thanks longer than two sentences or turning into a paragraph of praise, process or setup placed between the thanks and the answer, and the same thanks wording repeated across answers or across reviewer files. When the W/Q is a can / could / does question or a "cannot / does not" claim, the answer sentence must start with yes, the scoped form of yes, or "not yet". An argument, a setup, or a finding about a neutral side question fails, even if it implies the answer. So does a yes that appears only in a split reply's continuation comment ([rhetoric.md](rhetoric.md) § 1, "Can / cannot gets a yes or no first"; user 2026-11-12「有个能不能, 你都没说能不能」).
12. **Limitations-promise scan** — in each response's Limitations block, flag any promise-to-revise phrasing ("will be written into", "becomes an explicit statement", "we will state/name/separate in Limitations", "both become paper edits"). Fix: replace with the merits-based defense (`SKILL.md` Phase 4 "Limitations items are weaknesses") and move the edit to `REVISION_PLAN.md` marked `unpromised`.
13. **Comparison scan.** Per response file:
    - every baseline is compared on the same metric(s);
    - every favorable comparison has its paired interval on record in its issue card; when that interval includes 0, the file prints no interval and does not call the gap significant;
    - no tie is stated as a result;
    - no number can be lined up against a paper table to show the flagship losing;
    - every time or memory comparison has the same measurement setup for every arm (card, decoding and its parallelism, batching, caching).

    Fix per [comparison-rules.md](comparison-rules.md).
14. **Self-contradiction scan** — every evaluative word ("improves", "outperforms", "maintains", "consistent gains") is true of the numbers printed in the same reply, and no reply contradicts a number in the paper or in another thread. Fix the word, not the number.
15. **Foreign-content scan** — every section name, component name, dataset and number in a reply exists in this paper or in the user-confirmed evidence; flag text carried over from another project or template (in one thread, a reply to the negative reviewer named a section and a module that exist only in another project's paper).
16. **Unverified-cause scan** — any explanation of why a number, sample or result looks the way it does (hardware variance, seed, tokenizer, "noise") must cite the log or check that established it (`SKILL.md` Phase 3 step 6). A guessed cause is deleted.
17. **Measurement scan** — every reviewer ask for a measurable quantity (latency, FLOPs, memory, a count, a breakdown) is answered with that quantity, or the issue is `needs_user_input`; a derivation plus a camera-ready promise does not close it.
18. **Outlier-framing scan** — no reviewer thread compares its reviewer with the other reviewers ("unlike the other three reviewers"); that framing belongs only in the AC-only closing note ([posting-and-endgame.md](posting-and-endgame.md) § 2b).
19. **New-result framing scan** — every result not in the submitted paper is labelled new and preceded by its goal ("To test whether …"), with setup (models, sizes, data, what "zero-shot"/recipe means); every number carries its address (table/figure/appendix, or "a new analysis of the paper's released labels"; never "(no new labels)" or "(same labels)", [collaboration.md](collaboration.md) § 8); every "we ran / scored / computed" is followed by its result; every evidence paragraph ends with a conclusion sentence ([rhetoric.md](rhetoric.md) § 6, C1–C4).
20. **Readability scan** — nothing referred to before it is named ("both versions", "the recipe"); no drafting-time labels; no fragment answers ("It does."); future work names concrete methods; every comparison arm has a short name defined once (C18); no new term for a one-off statistic (C26); every item in a "differs in N ways" list is an advantage (C25) ([rhetoric.md](rhetoric.md) § 6–7, C5–C7, C9).
21. **Number-density scan** (user 2026-11-10: 「rebuttal 就不要写这么多具体数字, 最好搞个表」) — a paragraph that carries values for more than one benchmark or arm, or more than about four numbers, moves them into a table and keeps the verdict in prose (C17); a cost measurement leads with its operational consequence (one card vs. two, inputs completed) before GB or seconds (C20); a decision sentence ("We therefore use …") follows a summary of the numbers that justify it (C24).
22. **Secondary-evidence scan** (user 2026-11-10: 「次要证据如果别人没要就别给, 如果要了就给」) — every result in an answer either answers the reviewer's ask or is support a sentence of that review asks for; other support is deleted and held in the issue card as a follow-up answer, except the only support for a claim or what the user or a co-author asked for by name (C34).
23. **Voice scan** (user 2026-11-12: 「我们尽量用 We 这样子的主动语态」) — every action the authors took is written in active voice with "we" (or our method, a table, a result) as the subject: "We fine-tuned the forecaster on …", "Table 3 shows …", never "The forecaster was fine-tuned …", "It can be seen that …", "It should be noted that …". Passive stays only where the actor is unknown or irrelevant ("each image is labelled by three annotators"), and in frozen spans (reviewer quotes, quoted paper text, co-author sentences). The sentence after the thanks stays the verdict, not "We ran …" (lint 11). Grep in [`/paper-polish` references/rewrite.md](../../paper-polish/references/rewrite.md) § 1 ([rhetoric.md](rhetoric.md) C35).
24. **Deference-line scan** (user 2026-11-14) — grep every reply for "respect", "final judgment", "final decision" and their Chinese counterparts (尊重, 最终判断); delete any deference line such as "However, we fully respect your final judgment." ([round2-replies.md](round2-replies.md) § 7). A request for a higher rating is allowed and stays.
25. **Conventions scan** (user 2026-11-13) — a reply with three or more significance statements or intervals defines *significant* once in a `**Conventions.**` line at its opening, with the true resampling unit, and its body says "significant(ly)" instead of repeating "the 95% … interval excludes zero" (`SKILL.md` Phase 4 "Significance is defined once").
26. **AC-note rating-tag scan** (user 2026-11-19) — grep every note addressed to the AC for `(rating`, `(score` and a parenthesis opening with a digit right after a reviewer ID; delete the tag ([posting-and-endgame.md](posting-and-endgame.md) § 2b). Before the user posts the note, reload the forum and check each "has not responded" and each score against it.
