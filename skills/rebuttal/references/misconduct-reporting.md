# Bad-faith, LLM-generated or abusive reviews: how to report

Read when an issue card carries `reviewer_stance: bad_faith_suspected` (Phase 2), the user asks to report a reviewer, or a negative-score reviewer stays silent after its nudge ([round2-replies.md](round2-replies.md) § 6: the AC note uses the letter of § 4, except the last silent negative-score reviewer in the last days, who gets the short engagement note of § 6 there).
A report is an outward-facing act on the user's paper: **draft it, show it, and let the user send it.** Never post it yourself.

Policy sources (checked 2026-11-09; re-check the venue's current guide before each use):
- ICLR 2027 Author Guidelines — https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines
- ICLR 2027 Reviewer Guidelines — https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/ReviewerGuidelines
- ICLR 2027 AI Policy for Reviewers — https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AIPolicyForReviewers
- ICLR 2027 Dates — https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/Dates
- ICLR 2026 response to LLM-generated papers and reviews — https://blog.iclr.cc/2025/11/19/iclr-2026-response-to-llm-generated-papers-and-reviews/
- ICLR 2026 retrospective — https://blog.iclr.cc/2026/03/31/a-retrospective-on-the-iclr-2026-review-process/
- NeurIPS 2025 Responsible Reviewing Initiative — https://blog.neurips.cc/2025/05/02/responsible-reviewing-initiative-for-neurips-2025/

## 1. Strict is not malicious — triage first

Reporting a reviewer who is merely tough wastes the AC's goodwill and makes the authors look thin-skinned. The model of a **legitimate hard reviewer**: specific asks, re-reads the rebuttal, replies with a sharper criterion, credits what was fixed (one negative reviewer in a past thread did all four). That reviewer is answered with argument ([rhetoric.md](rhetoric.md) § 3), never reported.

Reportable, with the venue's wording where it exists:

| Signal | Venue basis |
|---|---|
| False claims about what the paper contains or does (says a table/experiment/baseline is missing when it is there; attributes a claim the paper never makes) | ICLR 2026: "Very poor quality reviews that feature false claims, misrepresentations or hallucinated references are also a code of ethics violation" |
| Hallucinated references (cited papers that do not exist, or exist but say something else) | Same; ICLR 2026 authors "should post a confidential message to ACs and SACs pointing out the poor quality reviews and provide the necessary evidence" |
| Review generated by an LLM without engagement with the paper (generic text that fits any paper, questions answered in the paper's abstract, wrong method name) | ICLR 2027 AI policy: "Any LLM-generated falsehoods, hallucinations, or misrepresentations in reviews, or reviews that are otherwise low quality, are subject to disciplinary action" |
| Placeholder / content-free review | ICLR 2027 Reviewer Guide: "Low quality reviews will be flagged by ACs and SACs, and the flagged reviewers will be warned"; NeurIPS 2025: "ACs and SACs will flag any low-quality review, such as placeholder ones" |
| Hostile, personal or demeaning language | ICLR 2026 AC guide: ACs "moderate the discussions so that they are not toxic" |
| Pressure, threats, collusion attempts, attempts to identify authors | ICLR 2026 retrospective: offending members were "banned and desk rejected" |
| Breach of confidentiality (paper content shared or fed to a non-private tool) | ICLR 2026 LLM policy: confidentiality breaches "could result in consequences such as desk rejection of all of the reviewer's submissions" |

Gray zone — not reportable on its own; answer on the merits and mention to the AC only as part of a larger pattern:
- Requests to cite specific papers. Answer "We will cite X" if relevant. Only when the requested papers are unrelated to the submission and the request is a condition for the score is it worth noting to the AC, factually, without guessing who wrote them.
- Score inconsistent with the text (strengths only, rating 1–2). Point it out in the AC note as an observation, not an accusation.
- Silence after the rebuttal. Not misconduct; handled by [round2-replies.md](round2-replies.md) § 6.

Never a basis for a report:
- An LLM-detector score alone. ICLR uses detectors only for triage and acts "only … if an AC or SAC identifies concrete evidence"; its retrospective notes their "accuracy is far from perfect". A detector number can be one line in the evidence pack, after the factual errors.
- A guess at the reviewer's identity or affiliation.
- Disagreement on significance or taste.

## 2. Evidence pack

Build it in `rebuttal/MISCONDUCT_<ReviewerID>.md` (internal, never pasted anywhere as is). One row per claim:

| # | Reviewer text (verbatim) | What the paper actually says | Location | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "The paper does not compare with X." | Table 3 compares with X on 4 benchmarks | Table 3, l. 241 | false claim |
| 2 | "As shown in [Smith et al. 2024, CVPR] …" | No such paper in DBLP / CrossRef / Semantic Scholar / venue proceedings | — | hallucinated reference |
| 3 | "The method uses a diffusion prior." | The method has no diffusion component | §3 | misrepresentation |

Rules:
- Every row quotes the review verbatim and points to a line, section, table or figure of the **submitted** version (not the revised one).
- References are checked against DBLP → CrossRef → Semantic Scholar → the venue's proceedings; record each lookup.
- Count the rows. Two or three solid false claims or hallucinated references beat a long list of soft ones. Drop any row the paper does not settle unambiguously.
- Nothing in the pack admits a weakness of the paper (Never concede / Never expose a weakness apply here too). If a reviewer's point is partly right, it does not go in the pack; it gets a normal reply.

## 3. Channel and timing

**ICLR (2027 rules):**
1. **Confidential comment to the AC on the submission's forum.** Author Guidelines: "Authors who encounter potential violations of the Code of Ethics, e.g., as part of the review or public discussion, should raise these issues in a private message to their papers' Area Chair through the open review interface." Set *Readers* to the Area Chairs (and Senior Area Chairs / Program Chairs if offered); **never** include reviewers.
2. **Program Chairs by email**, only if the AC does not resolve it: "In cases that cannot be resolved together with the Area Chair, please contact the Program Chairs through email. Prefix your title as [Authors-to-PCs]." ICLR's policy posts list program-chairs@iclr.cc; confirm the address in the current guide.
3. **Timing: last, after the positive-score reviewers are secured** (user 2026-11-15「AC 的举报信现在可以发了吗 举报那个一分 一般都是搞定所有正分审稿人 最后搞这个一分 那再等一等」). Send the note once every positive-score reviewer has confirmed or been left silent after their nudge, and the reported reviewer has had its own nudge ([round2-replies.md](round2-replies.md) § 6). The note's strongest support is the other reviewers' confirmed ratings and their engagement, which exist only once they have confirmed; a reported reviewer still silent by then adds its non-reply as a fact (§ 4). It must still go out inside the author–reviewer discussion (ICLR 2027: Nov 05 – Nov 18 '26 AoE; reviewer–AC discussion and meta-reviewing begin Nov 19; reviewers flag CoE concerns by Nov 18), so the AC can still prompt the reviewer. This replaces the earlier "send as soon as the evidence pack is solid".

**NeurIPS:** the 2025 reviewer acknowledgement states that "Area Chairs will be able to flag up Insufficient Reviews … [and] grossly irresponsible reviewers (including but not limited to possibly LLM-generated reviews)". Use the venue's AC-only comment, or the author note to the AC (NeurIPS forums can show an "Author Final Remarks" note); check that year's author guide for which one exists.

**Other venues (CVPR/ICCV/ACL/…)**: find the AC-only / confidential comment in the author guide; if none exists, email the PCs with the same pack. Record the channel used in `REBUTTAL_STATE.md`.

## 4. The note to the AC

One message, written like a normal letter (user 2026-11-12: 「怎么举报是需要学习的, 就正常写就行」), not a charge sheet. It asks the AC to weigh the review, not to punish anyone. Structure, in this order (user 2026-11-12: 「你先把其他正常的审稿人夸一遍，然后说他们怎么怎么好，然后 bring attention to reviewer xxx 然后后面就说事情」):

1. **Thank the AC, then praise the other reviewers by name, specifically.** Say what each one did well, with a short verbatim quote or the concrete thing they checked ("Reviewer A checks the fine-tuning gains against two specific tables"). Generic thanks ("all reviewers were helpful") does not count. Only reviewers whose review is normal; skip the one being reported. Add that they engaged actively in the discussion, naming who confirmed a rating or replied, when that is true (user 2026-11-15「说为什么其他审稿人都说很好，然后积极engage」).
2. **"We would like to bring to your attention Reviewer X's review"**, with its rating / soundness / confidence.
3. **The facts, numbered, most serious first**, one per point (user 2026-11-12, moving the anonymity point ahead of W4: 「把第二点提到第一点，这个比较重要」). Each point: the reviewer's verbatim words, what the submission contains and where.
4. **Support from the other reviewers, inside each point** (user 2026-11-12: 「最好能从别的审稿人那里找一点支持你的结论」). For every point, search the other reviews for text that backs it and quote it verbatim: a reviewer who lists as a strength the very evidence the reported review says is missing is the strongest support; next, the other reviewers' sub-scores on the dimension the reported review attacks (e.g. "the other four rate soundness 2 or 3; two state 'no substantive concerns about the paper's soundness'"). Never cite a reviewer who raised the same concern as the reported one, and do not mention a point where the other reviewers agree with it (answer it in the public thread only).
5. **Close**: if the reviewer has not replied to our response or to our follow-up, say so in one factual sentence with the dates ("Reviewer <ID> has not yet responded to our response of <date> or our follow-up of <date>."; user「就这个负分的意见有问题123，然后不回复」). Then ask the AC to take the points into account and to encourage the reviewer to engage, and, in the same close, to encourage every other reviewer who has not replied to respond before the discussion closes, each named with the dates (user 2026-11-16「ac 就催所有没回的人」; [round2-replies.md](round2-replies.md) § 6 item 6). A close that echoes something said earlier in the letter is fine (user 2026-11-15「重复没关系, 这叫首尾呼应」): do not trim it or flag it as redundant. Do not add a sentence about the review's other points ("We address the substance of the reviewer's other points (W3 …, W5 …) in our response"): "substance" grants that they have merit, and naming them points the AC at the places where the reviewer may be right (user 2026-11-13: 「感觉也不该说? 等于承认他还是个好人.」).

```
Title: Reviewer <ID>, <point 1> and <point 2>

Dear Area Chair,

Thank you for handling our submission. We are grateful to all reviewers
for their time, and would like to thank Reviewers <A>, <B> and <C> in
particular for reviews that engage closely with the paper: Reviewer <A>
<concrete thing they checked>; Reviewer <B> finds "<verbatim praise>";
Reviewer <C> <...>. <Only if true: Reviewers <A> and <B> also engaged
actively in the discussion and confirmed their ratings.>

We would like to bring to your attention <N> points in Reviewer <ID>'s
review. <Optional, only if true: The review rates soundness <s>; the
other reviewers rate soundness <x> to <y>; Reviewers <B> and
<C> state "<verbatim>".>

1. <Heading naming the complaint, e.g. "A factual inaccuracy in W4".> The
   review states "<verbatim absolute sentence>". We respectfully wish to point
   out that the review appears to have overlooked <fact> that the original
   submission PDF (<date>, before any rebuttal revision) reports:
   <Appendix X, Table Y (p. Z of the original submission)>. Reviewer <A> lists this
   evidence as a strength: "<verbatim>".
2. <...>

<Only if true: Reviewer <ID> has not yet responded to our response of
<date> or our follow-up of <date>.> We would be
grateful if you could take these points into account when weighing the
review, and encourage Reviewer <ID> to engage with them during the
discussion.

Thank you so much for your time and efforts in handling our submission!
We sincerely appreciate your guidance and look forward to the discussion.
```

Writing rules:
- Facts and locations only about the reported reviewer. No adjectives about them ("careless", "lazy", "malicious", "clearly AI"), no "violates"; quote the rule and the review and let the AC draw the conclusion.
- **No thanks or praise for the reported reviewer**, not even a softener such as "We sincerely thank Reviewer X for a careful review, which led to several of our new experiments, and we raise these points only so that …" (a co-author added this on 2026-11-13; user: 「也不应该留, 他就是乱打分」). Thanking the reviewer tells the AC the review was useful, which undercuts every point of the note.
- **No summary of the revision in the note.** A list of new experiments and changes ("we carefully considered each comment … made substantial changes") dilutes the points, tells the AC the paper changed a lot (the "too many changes, resubmit elsewhere" risk), and every item in it becomes a claim the AC can check against the revised PDF. The note carries only the points about the reported review; the revision is described in the public replies (user 2026-11-13, on a co-author's version that opened with a six-item revision summary: 「她的基本上都可以全删了」).
- **"A factual inaccuracy", never "a factual error"** (user 2026-11-15, after changing "error" to "inaccuracy" at posting:「不要用 "error"，就用 inaccuracy 好点」), in the title, the opening sentence and the heading. The complaint is still named (not "Our submission does not support this statement.", which says nothing about the review): the softer form no longer tells the AC what the complaint is (see the next rule but one).
- **Point it out respectfully** (user 2026-11-15「多用这种 we respectfully wish to point out that the review from Reviewer X appears to have misconstrued / overlooked …」): after the quote, "We respectfully wish to point out that the review appears to have overlooked <evidence> …" (evidence in the submission) or "…, which appears to misconstrue <the rule>" (a guideline the review gets wrong); bullet heads likewise ("They appear to have overlooked the same appendix evidence."). This replaces the 2026-11-12 "This is incorrect." The heading ("A factual inaccuracy in W<n>") and the closing sentence of the point ("W<n> therefore rests on a statement that the submission contradicts …") stay, so the complaint is still plain. Do not force it onto a point where neither verb is accurate (a review citing an arXiv version neither overlooks nor misconstrues anything).
- Praise is for the other reviewers and must be specific and true; quote their own words where possible. Comparing scores is allowed when it supports a point (step 4); never as "the others liked it, so this one is wrong".
- **Each point says plainly what the complaint is** (user 2026-11-12, on a W4 point that only listed tables: 「我没看出来你要投诉什么…你要指出这个是 factual error 吧 别的审稿人也有不同意见…写清楚」). When the review makes an absolute statement about the submission's content ("No evaluation of X on Y") that the submission contradicts, the heading says "A factual inaccuracy in W<n>", the quote is followed by "We respectfully wish to point out that the review appears to have overlooked …", the evidence follows with locations, and one sentence names the complaint: "W<n> therefore rests on a statement that the submission contradicts and that another reviewer reads differently." The supporting reviewer is introduced as "Reviewer <A> cites this same evidence as a strength of the paper: \"<verbatim>\"", not "reads the same evidence the opposite way" or "explicitly disagrees": that reviewer never saw the reported review, so only their praise of the evidence is a fact (user 2026-11-13 kept this one sentence from a co-author's rewrite: 「可以. 你就改这一句」) This replaces the earlier neutral-heading rule ("Unseen-region evaluations (W4)"). Quote only the review's absolute sentence, never its hedge that the main text could support (in the 2026-11-12 case the main text said every transfer test used one held-out region, so the review's "the reported evaluation does not establish …" was left out, while its headline "No evaluation of <the model> on unseen regions" was false because an appendix tests that model itself on unseen stations), and tell the user about that exposure before sending. Other wording stays factual: no adjectives about the reviewer, no "did not read".
- Do not write "LLM-generated" as a finding. If relevant, say "the review contains [N] references we could not locate and [M] statements that do not match the submission"; the venue's own policy text names the consequence.
- No request to remove the review or change the score, and **no direct, formal request to lower the review's weight** ("We respectfully ask that Reviewer X's review be given less weight"): authors cannot formally ask an AC for that (user 2026-11-15, deleting such a sentence after it had been added the same day). Ask that the points "be taken into account when weighing the review" and that the AC encourage the reviewer to engage; the weighing is the AC's call.
- **Title = the points**, e.g. "Reviewer <ID>, an anonymity concern and a factual inaccuracy in W4", with no submission-number prefix (the comment already sits on the submission's forum; user 2026-11-15「不需要 Submission <N>:」). Not a request (user 2026-11-15「标题改回原来的」, after a title naming the asks).
- **Closing line**: warm, in the style of the posted replies' closings, not a bare "Thank you for your time." (user 2026-11-15「太普通了」): "Thank you so much for your time and efforts in handling our submission! We sincerely appreciate your guidance and look forward to the discussion." No signature line ("The authors of Submission <N>"): the forum shows who posted, and the characters are better spent on the letter (user 2026-11-15).
- **A review that counts the absence of non-peer-reviewed work as a weakness** (a model, method or dataset that exists only as an arXiv preprint or a company blog post). Add one short paragraph inside the related point, not a numbered point of its own: "W<n> and Q<m> also count the absence of <X> and <Y> as a gap. Neither has appeared at a peer-reviewed venue, and the Reviewer Guidelines state that "arXiv is not considered a peer-reviewed venue" and that "the lack of such comparisons cannot be a basis for rejection." We nonetheless added <both>, with <new evidence>, in our response to the reviewer." (user 2026-11-15「就用这一句短的」). Say it is part of the weakness, never its whole when the weakness also argues something else (e.g. a systematic bias): the AC reads the review and catches a narrowed paraphrase. Check both quotes verbatim on the venue's current reviewer guide first. No release dates: a date-based contemporaneous window can cut against you for the older item. The last sentence turns a public "thank you for this point" in the reply into "not required, done anyway"; tell the user that the public reply and the note are both visible to the AC.
- Open warm, then ask gently (co-author on an AC letter, 2026-11-10: "too direct … add something to warm up … do not say 'ask' directly, use 'kindly ask'"). A first sentence that is the request reads as a complaint.
- Ask for more than a re-weighting of one score (same co-author: "it is just a soundness score … rarely see a letter asking for this alone"). The AC can act on engagement.
- **Evidence the review calls missing: say it was in the original submission, with page and date** (user 2026-11-12: 「明确 Table ⟨n⟩/⟨m⟩ 已存在于原始投稿版，并给出页码、版本日期。这样 AC 能分清"原稿中已有但未被讨论的证据"和"rebuttal 新补的实验"」). Write "The original submission PDF (<date>, before any rebuttal revision) already contains …", and give each table as "(p. N of the original submission)". Do not add "neither is a new experiment added for the rebuttal": the date and pages already say it (user 2026-11-12: 「没必要写」). Pin page numbers to the original submission, never to the revised PDF, so a revision uploaded first cannot make them wrong. Verify pages by rebuilding the submitted commit (`git archive <commit>` into a scratch dir, `latexmk`, read the labels from `main.aux`), not from a build of the current tree. Take the date from the venue's record (OpenReview forum → Revisions, original version); a commit date is only a fallback, and then tell the user to check it on the forum before sending.
- **Review-form instructions** are fair to cite (e.g. ICLR 2027 Critical Weaknesses: "identify one or two critical weaknesses … The goal is not to enumerate every negative point"), but as **one sentence at the end of the evidence point, not a numbered point of its own** (user 2026-11-12: 「第三点就简单点并在第一点」): "The review lists <k> weaknesses (W1 to W<k>) without stating which of them determine its rating of <r>; we would also be grateful if you could ask the reviewer which one or two do, as the review form's Critical Weaknesses field requests." No long quote of the form. Check the venue's public reviewer guide for wording that cuts the other way (ICLR 2027: "List strong and weak points of the paper. Be as comprehensive as possible.").
- **A review that cites a non-anonymous version (arXiv, project page).** Quote the review's words and the venue guide ("It is recommended that you ignore the version on arxiv."), and add that the venue is double blind when its guide says so (ICLR 2027 Author Guidelines: "ICLR 2027 is double blind, which means that all submitted papers should be anonymous."). Even when the user reads it as a rule violation (2026-11-12: 「直接违反规定了属于」), write "violates" only if a hard rule exists: re-checked 2026-11-12, ICLR 2027 Reviewer Guidelines, Author Guidelines and Code of Ethics contain no rule forbidding reviewers to read arXiv or look for authors; the reviewer-facing text is only the "recommended" line, so an AC who checks would overturn "violation". Put this point first when it is the most serious one, and tell the user why the word stays out. **Never acknowledge the non-anonymous version as yours**, not even to the AC: no "our arXiv submission", no "it is an earlier version", no list of what the submission adds over it (user 2026-11-12: 「你不要泄漏就行，你就说不能讨论」, reversing the same day's 「arxiv 版本也可以说是旧版本」; ICLR 2027 Author Guidelines FAQ: a near-identical arXiv version does not break anonymity "so long as you do not refer to it explicitly"). Write "The review refers to an arXiv version", then "To preserve anonymity, we do not discuss any non-anonymous version, here or in our response", and ask that the review be weighed against the submission itself; the evidence point shows what the submission contains. In the public reply, cut the reviewer's arXiv words out of any quote with "[...]" so the authors never repeat them in the thread. Never claim the reviewer reviewed that version, "searched for it", or guess who they are; never include a date that invites "why didn't you fix it before submitting".
- If the note goes out before your reply to that reviewer is posted, say so ("our response, which we will post in this thread"); never point the AC at a reply that is not there yet.
- One note per reviewer. A follow-up only if new evidence appears (e.g. the reviewer's reply adds a new false claim) — not to chase the AC.

## 5. The public thread, at the same time

- Still answer every point of that review in the public thread, on the merits, with the same rhetoric rules as any reviewer. The public reply is what the AC and other reviewers read; it must hold on its own.
- In public, correct a false claim with the location, flatly: "Table 3 reports the comparison with X (4 benchmarks)." No "the reviewer did not read", no "misunderstanding", no hint that a report exists.
- Never write in public that a review is LLM-generated, hallucinated or in bad faith.

## 6. Never

- Contact a reviewer outside OpenReview, or try to identify one. After the ICLR 2026 identity leak, "collusion attempts, threats, and harassment" led to bans and desk rejections of the offenders' submissions.
- Pressure for a score, in public or in private.
- Post the report with reviewers among the readers.
- Send a report the user has not read and approved.
- Let the report replace the rebuttal: the paper is decided on the public answers; the report only reduces the weight of a review that cannot be answered.

## 7. Record

- `ISSUE_BOARD.md`: the reviewer's cards get `reviewer_stance: bad_faith_suspected` and a pointer to `MISCONDUCT_<ID>.md`; each card still needs a public answer (coverage gate).
- `REBUTTAL_STATE.md`: date sent, channel, readers setting, AC response (if any).
